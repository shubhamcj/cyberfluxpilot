Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

The CyberFluxPilot assessment runs entirely in your browser. Your answers and results are not sent to our servers and we do not store them.

What stays on your device

Your answers may be held temporarily in your browser's memory while you complete the assessment. Closing the tab clears them. If you choose to print or save your report, that file is created on your device.

What we collect

  • Email you send us. If you contact us, we keep the message and your address to respond.
  • Basic hosting logs. Our hosting provider may record standard request data (such as IP address and user agent) for security and reliability.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use advertising trackers.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/UK you may request access to, correction of or deletion of any personal data we hold by emailing privacy@cyberfluxpilot.com.

Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.
